How to Play

  • Play as X vs CPU or invite a friend for multiplayer.
  • Choose match length: First to 3 or First to 5 wins.
  • Click a cell to place your mark. Three in a row wins the round.
  • Enter your name → click Multiplayer → type a room name.
  • Share the room name with your friend. First joiner becomes Host (X).
  • Built-in chat lives bottom-left. You can collapse or expand it anytime.
  • R — restart round (Host only in multiplayer).
  • Enter — send chat message (when chat input is focused).
  • Corners first is strong for X. Watch forks and blocks.
  • As O, mirror center/corner pressure and play for draws.
  • In matches, momentum matters—reset quickly between rounds.
