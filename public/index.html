How to Play

  • Play as X vs CPU or invite a friend for multiplayer match.
  • Choose match length: First to 3 or First to 5 wins.
  • Dont forget the most important thing.... HAVE FUN!!!
  • Enter your name → click Multiplayer → type any number or word as room name.
  • Share the room name with your friend (VERY IMPORTANT ROOM NAME HAS TO BE EXACT MATCH). First joiner becomes Host (X).
  • Built-in chat is available on the bottom-left. You can collapse or expand it anytime.
  • If one of the players in multiplayer mode close browser or tab game will end automatically.
  • R → Restart Round (Host only in multiplayer mode).
  • Enter → sends chat message (Only in multiplayer mode).
